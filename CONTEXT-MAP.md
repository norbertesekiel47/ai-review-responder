# Context Map

RepWorth's domain glossary is multi-context. Each context below is one file under `docs/context/`, capped at 3,000 words, and is the single owner of the bold terms it lists. A historical pointer of the form `CONTEXT.md → **Term**` resolves here: find the term in the list, open its file. Every `- **Term**: …` definition line moved verbatim from the former single-file `CONTEXT.md` (the seed glossary, L1–38 at the split, became the three `core-*` files; each `## ` section became its own file; the 7,200-word send-mode section is three parts).

## Relationships

- **Solicitation (watch-mode) → Solicitation (send-mode)**: send-mode extends the watch-mode entries and never replaces them; watch-mode reserved **Outreach** for send-mode's actual sends and deferred the batch `expired` state to send-mode, and both land in send-mode.
- **Solicitation contacts & visits → Solicitation suppression & unsubscribe**: the **Consent ledger** records what the owner asserted; the **Suppression ledger** records what the recipient revoked, and the second outranks the first, permanently (**Attestation never overrides suppression**).
- **Send-mode part 2 ↔ part 3**: the **Halt latch** stops ONE batch; the **Send breaker** stops ALL solicitation sending platform-wide, and it is never a per-batch state.
- **Restraint policy → Solicitation (watch-mode)**: an **Ask** is a send | withhold decision with a closed withhold-reason enum; the **Restraint policy** is the rule set that makes that decision and **Withhold reason** is the enum it draws from.
- **Core reviews → Core reviews**: the **Live-ingest cutoff** is the instant from which the live lane owns review handling — the recent poller consumes it as its lower bound and the **Review import** consumes it as the **Import window**'s upper bound.
- **Core reviews → Send-mode parts 2 and 3**: **Approval method** names the two ways a response earns posting (`owner` | `auto_policy`); **Owner approval** (`approval_method='owner'`) and the **Auto-approval lane** (`approval_method='auto_policy'`) are the solicitation send lane's two instances of it.
- **Refusal ledger ← solicitation and response lanes**: an **Ask** withheld at build, an Outreach withheld at send, a batch halted, and a draft a policy gate held are each a **Refusal**; an owner act (`owner_veto`, `owner_halt`, `owner_excluded`, `owner_withdrawn`) is the owner's decision and is not.
- **Admin invite funnel → Core accounts**: an **Admin invite** is an **Account** minted by staff, and the **Checkout attempt** is the **Account**-owned durable lease.
- **Owner Operator → Core reviews**: a **Proposal** never posts; a voice **Proposal** changes only future drafts.
