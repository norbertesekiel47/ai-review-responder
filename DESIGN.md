# Design System: RepWorth

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Warm Editorial Console"**

RepWorth has two deliberate visual registers under one brand. Public surfaces use the Island: generous white content fields floating on sage, with warm tactile accents. Authenticated owner surfaces use the Warm Editorial Console: a calmer, denser work surface built from cream paper, espresso ink, concentric trays, and restrained semantic color. Pick one register per surface and never mix their component grammar on the same screen.

The Console is designed for owner-operators checking reputation work between real-world tasks. It favors a two-second scan, plain status language, and quiet evidence over generic dashboard spectacle. Motion acknowledges state and continuity; it never delays the work. Product truth, especially the approval gate and agent run spine, is more important than decorative analytics.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Warm paper surfaces with espresso text and rare sage action color.
- Editorial display type paired with compact, highly legible UI type.
- Concentric tray-and-core cards that communicate structure without heavy chrome.
- Semantic colors that always retain a text, icon, shape, or positional cue.
- Desktop information density with a touch-ready mobile and hybrid-device path.

## Colors

The palette is warm, low-chroma, and role-driven. Sage indicates action and positive state; blush carries attention; terracotta is reserved for danger; violet identifies Insights provenance; gold belongs only to review ratings.

### Primary

- **Guardian Sage:** The Console action, focus, selected-navigation, and positive-state foreground. Use the darker actionable token for text and controls; the lighter Island teal is decorative only.

### Secondary

- **Approval Blush:** Attention, owner intervention, and Guardian-adjacent context. Pair the wash with its deep foreground.
- **Insights Violet:** Provenance for Insights and machine-origin state. It is not a general accent.
- **Review Gold:** Rating stars and rating evidence only. It must not become a warning or CTA color.

### Tertiary

- **Terracotta Warning:** Errors, destructive actions, and blocked states. Use the soft wash behind the accessible dark foreground.

### Neutral

- **Paper Field:** The authenticated application canvas.
- **Porcelain Card:** The card core and opaque floating surface.
- **Worktable Recess:** Inputs, draft panels, and quiet inset regions.
- **Espresso Ink:** Primary text and the strongest Console action.
- **Warm Muted Ink:** Secondary evidence and supporting copy.

### Named Rules

**The Two Registers Rule.** Island owns public surfaces; Warm Editorial owns authenticated surfaces. Never mix them within one screen.

**The Actionable Color Rule.** Decorative pale teal never carries text, links, or button labels. Interactive color must meet contrast in its actual state.

**The Evidence Color Rule.** Color never carries reputation status alone; pair it with copy, shape, iconography, or position.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Young Serif (Georgia fallback)
**Body Font:** DM Sans (system sans fallback)
**Label/Mono Font:** DM Sans for labels; the system mono stack only for identifiers, measurements, and code-like data.

**Character:** Young Serif makes the owner console feel cared for without turning operational UI into marketing. DM Sans keeps dense review, settings, and billing work direct and readable.

### Hierarchy

- **Display** (400, 24px, 1.2): Page titles and the largest reputation values.
- **Headline** (400, 18px, 1.3): Card and section headings.
- **Title** (600, 16px, 1.3): Functional subheads in DM Sans when emphasis must remain operational.
- **Body** (400, 14px, 1.5): Standard owner-console copy; explanatory text should stay within roughly 70 characters per line.
- **Label** (600, 12px, 0.04em): Compact status and field context. Uppercase is reserved for short factual labels.

### Named Rules

**The Honest Weight Rule.** Young Serif ships at one weight. Never synthesize bold; hierarchy comes from size, placement, and the DM Sans functional scale.

## Layout

The authenticated shell uses a fixed 240px desktop sidebar and a fluid main canvas capped by content-specific maximum widths. The dashboard content container reaches 1140px; settings stop at 880px; focused wizards stop at 640px. Spacing follows a 4px base rhythm, with 16–24px inside components and about 30px between major dashboard zones.

Below the shell breakpoint, the sidebar becomes a fixed bottom navigation, content collapses to one or two columns according to information density, and safe-area insets protect bottom actions. Tables may scroll horizontally when preserving column relationships is more useful than converting them to cards. All touch-capable devices, including hybrids, receive at least 44px interactive targets.

**The Two-Second Glance Rule.** The dashboard overview contains only the reputation state, the intelligence brief, and clear doorways to pending work and analytics.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is primarily structural: a warm tray surrounds an inner card core, with hairline borders and a subtle inset highlight. Shadows are warm, diffuse, and progressively stronger only for floating controls, dialogs, or an intentional hover response. Fixed top bars and save bars may use bounded backdrop blur because they reveal content passing beneath them.

### Shadow Vocabulary

- **Quiet Surface:** A small warm ambient shadow for cards at rest.
- **Raised Surface:** A longer diffuse shadow for hovered or layered cards.
- **Floating Surface:** The strongest warm shadow for dialogs and detached controls.
- **Inset Highlight:** A one-pixel inner light that completes the double-bezel material.

### Named Rules

**The Concentric Depth Rule.** Major cards use the tray-and-core relationship; do not stack an unrelated border, shadow, and glow to manufacture depth.

## Shapes

Operational controls use gently curved 6–8px corners. Cards use 12–20px concentric radii according to their tray depth. Pills are reserved for compact actions, filters, tabs, statuses, and small numeric badges. The 48px Island corner belongs to public island containers and never appears in the dense Console shell.

## Components

### Buttons

- **Shape:** Compact pills for dashboard actions and 8px corners inside settings workflows.
- **Primary:** Espresso fill for the strongest owner action; sage fill where selection or connection is the meaning.
- **Hover / Focus:** Short explicit color, border, shadow, or transform transitions; a visible sage focus ring; no broad transition of every property.
- **Secondary / Ghost / Tertiary:** Porcelain or transparent surfaces with espresso or muted text. Destructive actions use terracotta only.

### Chips

- **Style:** Small pills with a semantic wash and an accessible foreground.
- **State:** Selection changes fill, foreground, weight, and where useful a check or position—not color alone.

### Cards / Containers

- **Corner Style:** 14px core inside a 20px tray for major dashboard cards; simpler 12–16px cards for secondary workflows.
- **Background:** Porcelain card over a cream tray or paper field.
- **Shadow Strategy:** Quiet at rest, raised only when interaction or layering requires it.
- **Border:** Warm translucent hairlines.
- **Internal Padding:** Usually 16–24px, with tighter spacing inside evidence groups.

### Inputs / Fields

- **Style:** Recessed warm field, 8px radius, readable 14px text, and sufficient mobile focus sizing.
- **Focus:** Dark sage border with a three-pixel translucent halo.
- **Error / Disabled:** Terracotta copy on an accessible wash for errors; muted ink and a recessed surface for disabled controls.

### Navigation

Desktop navigation is a quiet left rail with icon, label, and a slim active marker. Mobile navigation is a fixed bottom bar with safe-area padding. Both use `aria-current`, persistent text labels, and the same information architecture.

### Reputation Evidence

Charts provide a textual summary or data table, not just graphical marks. Rating stars use Review Gold with an accessible label. Synthetic trends are forbidden: when historical data does not exist, show the value without a fabricated sparkline.

### Dialogs

Use native modal dialogs for consequential settings actions. The dialog owns its full-viewport dismissal surface, Escape behavior, focus containment, and accessible name. Every destructive modal states the consequence and presents explicit cancel and confirm actions.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** make the owner’s current reputation state and next action obvious within seconds.
- **Do** use named RepWorth roles—Reply, Guardian, Insights, Judge, Governor—and preserve their actual capabilities.
- **Do** resolve reduced-motion experiences directly to the final visual state while preserving immediate feedback.
- **Do** use `any-pointer: coarse` when adapting targets for devices that can accept touch.
- **Do** keep public Island and authenticated Console tokens single-sourced in their owning stylesheets.

### Don't:

- **Don't** fabricate trends, metrics, testimonials, customers, or agent activity.
- **Don't** use pale decorative teal for text or controls.
- **Don't** introduce purple-blue AI gradients, black glass, orbital decoration, or generic chatbot imagery.
- **Don't** use a colored side border as generic card decoration; borders may distinguish a real comparison or system state only when the structure requires it.
- **Don't** animate layout-driving properties or leave broad `transition: all` declarations in production UI.
- **Don't** use emoji or Unicode symbols as substitutes for the established inline SVG icon system.
