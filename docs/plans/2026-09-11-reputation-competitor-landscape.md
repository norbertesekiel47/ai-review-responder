# Reputation competitor evidence: NiceJob, TrueReview, GatherUp

Checked: 2026-09-11. Scope: publicly documented owner interaction, AI automation, and international coverage. This is a desk review of first-party product/help/API/changelog pages, not a hands-on trial or a verification of vendor performance claims. “True Reviews” is interpreted as **TrueReview**, the local-business reputation product at truereview.co. Birdeye and Podium are outside this report's scope.

## Findings that affect RepWorth's positioning

All three vendors document AI review replies and background automation. NiceJob also documents an owner describing a broadcast to AI and asking it to refine the result. A conversational interface and multilingual text generation alone therefore do not establish uniqueness. The narrower opportunity to validate is an owner delegating a reputation objective, approving an executable plan, and having the system operate and revise that plan across the owner's actual channels. The sources reviewed do not establish whether these vendors offer that complete experience; absence from this review is not proof of product absence.

International availability must be compared per capability. TrueReview explicitly supports email requests outside North America while restricting SMS. GatherUp claims international agency use and documents multiple business languages. No evidence here supports describing this whole competitor group as exclusive to the US or EU.

## NiceJob

**Owner interaction and AI:** The current help article for Broadcasts, dated May 9, 2026, describes writing a request to its AI content generator, selecting language/tone/length, and asking AI to refine a draft. The owner then adds a CTA, chooses recipients, and sends or schedules. Broadcasts are email-only, one message per broadcast, and available on Pro. This is conversational content creation within a prescribed workflow; the article does not demonstrate autonomous configuration of the owner's entire reputation program. [Broadcasts help](https://help.nicejob.com/en/articles/14758583-win-back-customers-with-broadcasts).

**Background action:** AI Review Replies lets owners configure tone, voice, length, and which reviews receive automatic replies. Its help page says it processes the prior 24 hours around 6 pm Eastern each day and does not reply to older historical reviews. [AI Review Replies help](https://help.nicejob.com/en/articles/10324617-ai-review-replies).

NiceJob also documents a Get Repeats campaign triggered by booking, invoice, or payment integrations. Owners configure when the campaign starts after a service; the message sequence is predefined and the help page says its internal timing is not customizable. [Get Repeats help](https://help.nicejob.com/en/articles/9175838-get-more-repeat-business).

**International evidence and limits:** The Broadcasts article explicitly has language selection, but does not enumerate supported languages or certify their quality. A messaging guide covers Canada, the US, Australia, and New Zealand; that is evidence of regional operational documentation, not a current exhaustive availability matrix or legal guidance to reuse. Gifting is explicitly limited to US/Canada companies and recipients, a limit on that feature alone. Full country coverage, language quality, sender provisioning, and localization of the owner interface remain unverified. [Regional messaging guide](https://help.nicejob.com/en/articles/5952360-messaging-consent-by-region), [gifting limits](https://help.nicejob.com/en/articles/6930802-gifting-with-nicejob).

## TrueReview

**Owner interaction and AI:** The May 1, 2026 help page documents automated responses to Google, Facebook, and TrueReview reviews. Owners set rating bands, timing, AI versus templates, tone, and handling for reviews in other languages. AI responses can follow the language of the original review. Manual posting before a scheduled reply cancels that scheduled reply. This is already bounded automation with owner controls. [Automated replies help](https://truereview.help/en/articles/11150094-enable-automated-review-replies).

**Adjacent capability:** Review Radar is advertised as scanning incoming Google reviews for possible policy violations and recommending reporting categories. This supports an AI-assisted reputation-defense offering; it is not a verified guarantee of removal. [Review Radar announcement](https://www.truereview.co/post/introducing-review-radar-google-review-policy-violation-detection).

**International evidence and limits:** The explicit country-support article, dated June 27, 2023 and still accessible when checked, says SMS requests support businesses in the US and Canada; email requests support all locations. Because the page is old, verify with the vendor before treating it as a procurement guarantee. Its existence directly contradicts a blanket claim that TrueReview is unusable outside North America. [Country support](https://truereview.help/en/articles/8059503-which-countries-does-truereview-work-in).

No owner-facing conversational setup/operator spanning workflows was established from the reviewed product and help documentation. Exact supported languages, translated owner UI, and target-country deliverability remain unverified. Some marketing articles emphasize human approval despite the explicit auto-reply help page; prefer the feature-specific help page for the documented controls rather than treating the marketing prose as a universal restriction.

## GatherUp

**Owner interaction and AI:** AutoReply documents automatic AI responses to positive Google and Facebook reviews. Auto Reply Templates additionally allows up to 50 owner-written templates, with AI selecting a suitable response and configurable rating thresholds. These are owner-configured automated workflows. [AutoReply changelog](https://changelog.gatherup.com/autoreply-automatic-review-responses-287341), [template controls](https://changelog.gatherup.com/auto-reply-templates-311887).

GatherUp also documents AI suggestions in email notifications that owners can approve without entering its dashboard, custom reply prompts, and Smart Insights across direct feedback and third-party reviews. This means a low-dashboard, proactive owner experience is already part of its documented offering. [Expanded AI capabilities](https://changelog.gatherup.com/expanded-ai-capabilities-for-first-party-feedback-313630).

**International evidence and limits:** Its FAQ says agencies use GatherUp around the world. Its publicly accessible business-create API documentation lists language codes `de`, `en`, `es`, `fr`, `nl`, `no`, `pl`, `pt`, `sv`, and `it`. This documents a multilingual business setting; it does not prove that every UI, AI capability, or messaging channel is localized to those languages. [FAQ](https://gatherup.com/faq/), [business-create API](https://app.gatherup.com/api/doc/business/create).

An older 2017 release note describes customers using SMS in the UK and Australia. Treat that only as historical evidence against an assumption of permanent US exclusivity; the current full SMS country matrix was not established. The main help site returned a loading/CSS error during this review, limiting verification of current detailed support rules. [Historical SMS release note](https://gatherup.com/blog/release-notes-may-2017/).

No owner-facing conversational setup/operator spanning workflows was established from the reviewed sources. GatherUp's website chat offering is described as web-to-SMS customer communication, which is a different interaction from the owner's proposed reputation operator. [Product features](https://gatherup.com/solutions/).

## Product hypothesis to test

RepWorth could compete on a complete outcome: an owner explains their business and constraints in their own language; the system identifies supported connections, configures an approved operating plan, executes through durable domain actions, surfaces only decisions the owner needs to make, and measures whether the reputation issue improved. That is a product hypothesis, not a proven market gap. Validate it with direct competitor trials and a small target segment before promising global parity or building broad channel coverage.

Useful trial questions: Can an owner change several related policies in ordinary language? Does the system preview consequential changes, preserve its operating instructions, recover from incomplete setup, and proactively revise a plan using observed outcomes? Can a business in a specific country complete connection, consent, delivery, public reply, escalation, and owner approval in the language/channel it actually uses? Measure these journeys rather than counting AI features.
