# Brand Voice

## When to use

Use this when an owner wants replies to sound different or asks why drafts do not match their preferred style.

## Customer reply

Hi {{owner_name}},

RepWorth uses your Brand Voice settings when drafting new replies. You can update tone, preferred phrases, business facts, and things to avoid from Settings. After updating the voice, regenerate any existing draft that should use the new style.

Send us an example reply you like if you want help tuning the voice.

## Internal checks

- Confirm the owner updated the correct location or account-level voice.
- Check whether the draft was created before the latest voice change.
- Review avoid-facts and sample responses for contradictions.
- Regenerate one draft only after the owner approves the direction.

## Escalation

Escalate if the model includes prohibited claims, private information, unsafe medical/legal language, or repeated facts from the avoid list.
