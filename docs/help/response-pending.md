# Response Pending

RepWorth drafts a reply after a new Google review is ingested. Most drafts appear within a few minutes.

Check these states before contacting support:

- The review is visible in the dashboard.
- The account subscription is active or still inside the past-due recovery window.
- Google Business publishing is connected.
- The reply is not already awaiting owner approval.

If a response is still pending after 30 minutes, contact support with the business name, reviewer name, and approximate review time.
