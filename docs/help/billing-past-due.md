# Billing And Past-Due Recovery

When payment fails, RepWorth keeps the account writable during the recovery window shown in the dashboard.

During the recovery window:

- Review approvals, edits, and publishing continue to work.
- Owners should update payment from the billing portal.
- Support can grant a short extension when there is an active billing ticket.

After the recovery window ends, write actions are paused until payment is updated. Data is preserved.
