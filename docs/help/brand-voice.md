# Brand Voice

Brand voice controls the tone and facts RepWorth uses when drafting replies.

Good inputs:

- Preferred tone, such as warm, concise, premium, or direct.
- Products, services, team names, and local details that are safe to mention.
- Facts to avoid, such as discounts, legal claims, guarantees, or private customer details.
- Sample replies that match the owner’s preferred style.

Changes apply to future drafts. Existing drafts should be regenerated if they need the updated voice.
