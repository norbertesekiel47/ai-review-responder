# Legal content cleanup — DPA stub + leaked AI-disclosure microcopy

- **Date:** 2026-05-29
- **Audit item:** `docs/launch-ready-part2.md` → Prioritized fix list → P0 #11
  ("Gate/remove the DPA placeholder and the AI-disclosure strings in Privacy/AUP").
- **Scope:** Content-only edits to `src/lib/legal/content.ts` + a regression test.
  Worktree/PR #1 of the two requested fixes.

## Problem

Two distinct defects live in the typed legal documents (`src/lib/legal/content.ts`,
rendered by the already-branded `src/components/legal/LegalPage.tsx`):

1. **Leaked UI microcopy in legal prose.** The product's AI-draft disclosure
   sentence — `"Draft generated by AI. Review carefully before approving."` —
   has a single authoritative owner: `AI_DRAFT_DISCLOSURE` in
   `src/lib/ui/disclosures.ts`. It is meant for in-product approval surfaces
   (the public approval page and the in-app drawer) and legitimately appears
   there. Three hardcoded copies of that imperative microcopy have leaked into
   legal prose where it reads as a non-sequitur:
   - `content.ts:177` — Privacy → "How We Use Data": the string is the *sole*
     body paragraph, followed by the real bullet list. Privacy also already has
     a dedicated, honest **"AI Draft Generation"** section, so this line is both
     misplaced and redundant.
   - `content.ts:259` — AUP → "Review and Approval Responsibility": prepended to
     the proper sentence "Owners are responsible for reviewing each draft…".
   - `content.ts:97` — Terms → "AI-Generated Content": the *identical* defect,
     prepended to "Response drafts are suggestions only…". The audit named only
     Privacy/AUP, but the same paste-error is in Terms; cleaning all three in
     one pass keeps the file consistent (one defect class, one file).

2. **Misleading DPA placeholder is live.** `/legal/dpa` is reachable by direct
   URL (it is **not** linked from any nav/footer) and currently states a
   lawyer-reviewed DPA "is pending" and that "customers should not treat this
   page as a completed legal addendum" (`content.ts:279–281`). Publishing an
   admission of incompleteness is a launch risk.

## Decisions (from brainstorming)

- **DPA:** Reword to a clean "available on request" stub — **keep** the
  `/legal/dpa` URL (B2B buyers look for it), **remove** the "pending / do not
  treat as complete" language.
- **Terms line 97:** Fix it too (one-pass cleanup), keeping the proper
  "drafts are suggestions only" sentence.

## Changes

All edits are in `src/lib/legal/content.ts`. No route, component, or styling
changes; `LegalPage` renders the typed sections as-is.

### 1. Remove leaked AI-disclosure microcopy (3 sites)

| Location | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Privacy → "How We Use Data" (`:177`) | `body: ['Draft generated by AI. Review carefully before approving.']` | `body: []` (bullets unchanged) |
| AUP → "Review and Approval Responsibility" (`:258–261`) | body = `[<microcopy>, <owner-responsibility sentence>]` | body = `[<owner-responsibility sentence>]` |
| Terms → "AI-Generated Content" (`:95–100`) | body = `[<microcopy>, <suggestions-only sentence>]` | body = `[<suggestions-only sentence>]` |

The product UI surfaces that legitimately show the disclosure
(`AI_DRAFT_DISCLOSURE`, approval-client, past-replies) are untouched.

### 2. Reword the DPA stub (`content.ts:271–296`)

- Rename the first section heading `"Status"` → **`"Requesting a DPA"`**.
- Replace its body with truthful on-request copy:
  - `"RepWorth provides a Data Processing Addendum to customers who require one for their compliance needs."`
  - `"To request our DPA, email info@repworth.net and we will provide the current addendum for your review and signature."`
- Leave the `"Processing Scope"` and `"Sub-processors"` sections unchanged
  (accurate and useful).
- `version` / `effectiveDate` (shared module constants) unchanged.

### 3. Regression test (`src/lib/legal/__tests__/content.test.ts`)

Add assertions (the existing two tests assert none of this, so nothing breaks):

- Import `AI_DRAFT_DISCLOSURE` from `@/lib/ui/disclosures`.
- For `privacy`, `aup`, and `terms`: no section's `body[]` entry equals
  `AI_DRAFT_DISCLOSURE`.
- For `dpa`: the concatenated body text contains **none** of `"pending"`,
  `"should not treat"`, and **does** contain `"on request"`/`"request our DPA"`
  and `"info@repworth.net"`; the `"Status"` heading is gone and
  `"Requesting a DPA"` is present.
