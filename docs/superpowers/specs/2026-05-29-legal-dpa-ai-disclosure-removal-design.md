# Design — Remove DPA placeholder + misplaced AI-disclosure strings (Launch P0 #11)

**Date:** 2026-05-29
**Branch:** `worktree-fix+launch-p0-legal-placeholders` (off `origin/main`)
**Audit item:** `docs/launch-ready-part2.md` → Prioritized fix list → P0 #11
("Gate/remove the DPA placeholder and the AI-disclosure strings in Privacy/AUP").
Related copy-bug lines: `launch-ready-part2.md:124-125`.

## Problem

Two distinct content defects, both rooted in the single authoritative legal copy
file `src/lib/legal/content.ts`:

1. **Misplaced AI-disclosure microcopy.** The approval-screen UI string
   `"Draft generated by AI. Review carefully before approving."` was pasted into
   legal prose as section body text in two places where it does not belong:
   - **Privacy → "How We Use Data"** (`content.ts:177`) — it sits as the lead
     `body` line above the actual data-usage bullets. The Privacy doc already
     carries the substantive disclosure in its dedicated **"AI Draft Generation"**
     section (`content.ts:189`).
   - **AUP → "Review and Approval Responsibility"** (`content.ts:259`) — it sits
     above the real owner-responsibility statement (`content.ts:260`), which
     already states the obligation in proper prose.

   This duplicates UI microcopy into legal documents (a one-authoritative-owner
   violation) and reads as a paste error in published legal text.

2. **Self-admitted DPA placeholder.** `/legal/dpa` renders `legalDocs.dpa`, whose
   "Status" section (`content.ts:280-281`) openly states *"A lawyer-reviewed DPA
   is pending… customers should not treat this page as a completed legal
   addendum."* The route is **orphaned** — no link anywhere in the codebase points
   to `/legal/dpa` (verified by grep) — yet it remains publicly reachable by
   direct URL, exposing placeholder legal text pre-launch.

## Decision

- **AI-disclosure strings:** remove the two misplaced lines. The substantive
  disclosure already lives in the proper sections, so no information is lost.
- **DPA:** remove the route entirely (chosen over gating-behind-a-flag or writing
  interim "available on request" copy). The page is already orphaned and
  self-admittedly not a real legal document; deletion is the lowest-risk
  launch-blocker fix and re-adding a real DPA later is a few lines. A feature flag
  for a one-time legal artifact would be unjustified YAGNI per repo rules.

## Changes

### `src/lib/legal/content.ts` (authoritative owner)

1. Privacy "How We Use Data" section: `body: ['Draft generated by AI. Review
   carefully before approving.']` → `body: []`. Bullets and all other sections
   unchanged. "AI Draft Generation" section remains the authoritative disclosure.
2. AUP "Review and Approval Responsibility" section: drop the first `body` line
   (the AI microcopy); keep the owner-responsibility statement intact.
3. Delete the entire `dpa: { … }` entry from `legalDocs` (`content.ts:271-297`).
4. Remove `'dpa'` from the `LegalDoc['slug']` union type (`content.ts:14`).

### Route

5. Delete `src/app/(public)/legal/dpa/page.tsx` and its now-empty `legal/dpa/`
   directory. Result: `/legal/dpa` returns a Next.js 404. No links point to it,
   so there are no broken links to repair.

## Out of scope (deliberately)

- No DB change, no migration, no env var, no feature flag.
- No UI/brand redesign — `LegalPage` renderer is untouched; the RepWorth
  brand-design skill is not needed (this is legal text, not new UI).
- The other Privacy/AUP/legal sections and the `sub-processors`/`cookies`/`terms`
  docs are not modified.
- The standalone top-level `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/security` marketing pages are
  separate from the `/legal/*` typed docs and are out of scope.
