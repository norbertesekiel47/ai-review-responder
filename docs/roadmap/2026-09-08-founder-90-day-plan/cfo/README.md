# CFO — 90-day founder brief

**Period:** September 8–December 6, 2026, inclusive: 90 days across 13 reporting weeks.
**Owner:** CFO. **Planning capacity:** 40 hours/week; up to 60 only when explicitly chosen for a temporary peak.
**Status:** Every task below is planned. This brief is not evidence that a forecast, investor contact, customer decision or receipt already exists.

**Strategy revision: September 11.** Add operator experiment economics to the
existing register when selected; retain the cash gates and current packages.

Read the [shared plan](../README.md) first. It owns the common scorecard definitions, acceleration gate, approval scope and founder handoffs. This brief supplies the CFO work needed for those decisions; it does not introduce a separate growth gate.

## Your job

Keep the company able to meet its commitments, turn its funding request into a credible financial case, and help the CMO find and evaluate customers. Give the CEO decisions they can make before cash runs out, with numbers they can trace back to evidence.

There are four founders: the CEO/CTO owns product and engineering; the COO prepares pitches and pitches alongside the CEO; you own financial evidence and supply it to the COO; the CMO owns messaging and channel selection. Your help to the CMO is an explicit part of the job, not spare-time work.

The September 7 planning inputs included estimated one-to-two-month runway, zero paying customers and approximately five interested prospects. Refresh those dated inputs from canonical records. The September 10 [decision log](../ceo-cto/decision-log.md) reports finance inputs supplied privately; complete obligations and the final cash deadline remain pending there. This brief does not re-audit those figures. Select the first customer group from the real roster; interested prospects must not be relabeled as active or paying beta accounts.

The [August 30 service snapshot](../../2026-08-30-operating-cost-baseline.md), interpreted on September 7, totals $729.17 in mixed plans, estimates and caps. It is not current burn, a live bill or a global spending limit. Its $399.17 subscription allowance is not the company's complete cash requirement. Do not use either number as the opening balance or a shortcut to runway.

The shared plan alone owns customer targets and their revisions. Its Day-30 learning target concerns paid yes decisions sought from the existing roster; the CFO records actual cash separately. Day-60/90 targets are set on October 7 from observed evidence. Do not turn a yes, a target or an upside case into forecast cash.

## What authority this brief gives you

Prepare analyses, drafts, source requests and decision packets. Obtain CEO approval before messaging anyone, submitting applications, spending, moving money, changing billing, entering financing terms or making other commitments. A budget draft is not spending authority. A document index is not legal clearance.

Reuse a recorded approval only within its specified recipients/segment, channel, purpose, message, cadence and budget; changed scope needs a new decision. COO coordinates the overall investor pipeline and legal review. You supply financial records, verify financial stages and flag money-related blockers.

Keep bank statements, cap-table details, contracts, personal finances and customer records in an agreed private location outside Git. The roadmap receives sanitized totals, assumptions, decisions and links that authorized founders can open. Do not put credentials or private immigration records here. Qualified counsel handles legal and immigration conclusions; raising capital does not guarantee an immigration outcome.

Expand the same financial record when a real decision requires it: dated forecasts, financing scenarios, pitch extracts and diligence views are incremental views, not week-1 paperwork prerequisites. Task IDs organize work within the CFO workstream; they do not create separate workstreams or require separate deliverable files.

## Task register and sequence

### CFO-01 — Reconcile cash and obligations

Start September 8; maintain whenever a balance or commitment changes. Collect company cash, restricted balances, receivables, payables, payroll/contractors, vendor renewals, taxes, debt, legal costs, refund exposure and founder support the company is expected to fund. Ask each founder for the binding date and approved aggregate support need; their personal budget need not be shared in this repository.

Separate invoice date, service period, due date and actual settlement date. Include annual renewals on their actual payment date in the cash forecast, even when a monthly report amortizes them. Label source reliability: payment reconciled, invoice confirmed, estimate, cap or unknown. Opening cash already contains past receipts; do not add them again as forecast income.

On September 8, explicitly calculate the **no-new-money operating floor**: the smallest dated costs that honor existing beta, service and other obligations plus the minimum founder support actually needed. Use opening cleared unrestricted cash and no future funding or uncollected revenue. Include essential service costs, cancellation/transition costs and obligations that cannot yet be reduced. Show proposed reductions separately from approved reductions and their effective dates; an unapproved cut cannot make the executable floor affordable.

Show current commitments and the approved floor side by side: cash required by date, cash covered, the first uncovered obligation and the decision date after the agreed buffer. If even the floor is unaffordable, flag that explicitly for an immediate founder decision. Do not assume unpaid founder labor, cancelled contracts or a financing receipt will solve it.

**Deliverable:** source fields and a sanitized summary in the one cash-sheet record. **Accepted when:** balances reconcile, obligations and minimum support are dated, the no-new-money floor and its coverage/decision dates are calculable, and unresolved differences are explicit. Missing inputs keep this task incomplete.

### CFO-02 — Maintain the dated 13-week cash forecast

The September 8 cash sheet must already answer the immediate coverage/deadline question. From September 9, extend that same record toward September 8–December 6 as the next operating decision, financing discussion or diligence request requires; do not build a second workbook just to complete this task. Preserve every known cash-event date, with weekly summaries and daily detail around the cash squeeze or large payments. Keep original currencies and disclose conversion assumptions.

| Scenario | What belongs in it | What it answers |
|---|---|---|
| Current commitments | Opening cleared unrestricted cash and all existing obligations; future customer receipts shown separately by confidence | What becomes unaffordable if no new funding arrives? |
| No-new-money floor | Opening cleared cash only; smallest operation honoring commitments and minimum founder support; approved reductions by effective date; proposed cuts shown separately | Is even the executable floor affordable, and until which exact date? |
| Conditional funding/growth | Named hypothetical receipts and dates, hiring/channel commitments, fees, support and delivery costs | What becomes possible if money clears, and what fails if it is late or absent? |

For each date: **closing cash = opening cash + receipts settling that date − outflows due that date**. A receipt confidence score is not cash. Show a version without future receivables alongside any version relying on contractual receipts. Do not average away a missed payment within a week.

Ask the CEO to choose the next decision period, cash reserve and explicit lead-time buffer using actual obligations; do not impose an arbitrary number of months of cash. The company cash-needed date is the first date available money cannot cover a due obligation. **Continuation decision date = earlier of the company cash-needed date and a binding founder support deadline, minus the agreed buffer.** Also flag any earlier non-cancellable commitment date. If any required input is unknown, the decision remains unevaluable.

**Deliverable:** expanded dated rows, assumptions and variance notes in the existing cash record. **Accepted when:** the decision-triggered scope ties to CFO-01, formulas reproduce balances, earlier obligations are covered, and the CEO can see the date/amount gap. The 13-week calendar is a planning view, not permission to fund 13 weeks.

### CFO-03 — Size the funding ask and use of proceeds

For a first financing conversation by September 10, supply the known cash gap and clearly labeled ask assumptions from the cash sheet. Expand only when a concrete financing decision or diligence request needs it. Compare the minimum bridge to a defined milestone, the affordable floor and a larger milestone-based plan within the same record. Amounts and horizons are outputs of dated costs, not round-size guesses or a prerequisite to first approved contact.

For a single upfront injection, the net requirement is the largest reserve shortfall across the selected horizon, with a floor of zero. Add funding costs to calculate gross proceeds needed. For a later receipt, separately test all obligations before it arrives. Show what each funded role, vendor or acquisition allocation buys, when its cost starts, and what evidence will justify the next allocation.

Compare possible routes—paid continuation, identified investors, suitable programs, or other founder-approved alternatives—by net amount, evidenced clearing date, conditions, obligations and capacity. Unknown dates stay unknown. Reduced outflows are not funding receipts. Do not assume a service-work buyer, personal loan or warm investor exists.

Model financing alternatives and dilution only from explicit assumptions or proposed terms. Include existing shares, grants, promised equity and convertible instruments when applicable; show future-round/option-pool scenarios separately. Have appropriate counsel verify legal mechanics and document requirements. The CFO does not choose or execute an instrument from a spreadsheet alone.

**Deliverable:** decision-triggered ask/use-of-proceeds and terms/ownership views of the existing record. **Accepted when:** each requested amount ties to dated costs and a milestone, downside exposure is visible, and assumptions are distinct from agreed terms. YC's [seed fundraising guide](https://www.ycombinator.com/blog/how-to-raise-a-seed-round/) supports linking an ask to a believable plan and showing different funding cases; its historical market figures are not model inputs.

### CFO-04 — Put the September 14 paid-decision target on financial footing

With CMO and COO, document the actual offer, who can decide, existing beta promises, price, start date, cancellation/refund terms and delivery responsibility. Seek a specific paid continuation decision from reachable eligible beta users by September 14 through approved human communication. That is a target for decisions, not a guaranteed number of payments. A promised free period is honored.

Record accepted, declined, undecided and unreachable separately, with the reason and next date. Distinguish invoice issued, future paid start, signed commitment and cash cleared. Do not turn a small subscription count into an assumed bridge for the whole company.

**Deliverable:** verified receipt/offer-cost fields in the CFO record, linked to COO's customer decision and renewal entries by account ID. **Accepted when:** decisions have inspectable evidence and cash claims match settlement evidence; no parallel customer-decision register is created.

### CFO-05 — Supply pitch financials and verify funding progress

For first approved conversations on September 8–10, give COO the known balance, gap, ask assumptions and source references inside the existing contact/approval packet. An expanded financial pitch or FAQ is prepared only when an actual meeting needs it, using the same cash record. COO owns assembling the pitch; CEO and COO own delivering it. Replace outdated figures before each consequential meeting.

Use the COO-owned funding tracker and the master's shared stages, adding financial detail for fit checks, term sheets and executed documents/conditions pending. CFO verifies amounts, terms assumptions and cash clearance; COO owns each investor's next action. Store actual dates, requested proof, conditions, next owner/date and sanitized evidence. No invented conversion rates, probability-weighted cash or forecast closing date. An investor-event theme is not an objection from an investor who assessed RepWorth.

**Deliverable:** financial extracts only as needed, and links from the cash record to verified financial stages in COO's pipeline. **Accepted when:** COO can reproduce the numbers, contradictions are resolved, and every advanced financial state has evidence. Provide week-1 feedback without letting investor preference automatically dictate features; do not create a parallel pipeline.

### CFO-06 — Prepare ownership and counsel review materials

Start by September 10. Inventory entity/jurisdiction records, ownership and issuance records, founder agreements, IP assignments, existing obligations, prior funding and any promised options. Unknown or absent records become questions for the responsible founder and qualified counsel. Link the existing [legal-review tracker](../../../legal/legal-review.md), not an invented clearance statement.

Give the COO a proposed financial/legal review scope with the document list, questions, deadline and a fee estimate only if sourced; COO coordinates counsel under approved authority. Financial readiness includes knowing which documents or approvals affect accepting funds; it does not imply pending website review prevents every investor conversation. Keep immigration assessment separate with qualified counsel.

**Deliverable:** finance-related questions and source references in COO's contact/counsel approval packet; expand a private document index only for an actual review/diligence need. **Accepted when:** owners and next dates are recorded, counsel conclusions are distinct from questions, and existence is not confused with approval.

### CFO-07 — Give the CMO useful research and acquisition economics

Reserve about 12 hours/week within the 40-hour plan, with urgent cash decisions taking priority through explicit reprioritization. CMO chooses the customer criteria, message and channel; you improve the list, measurement and financial decision.

- Validate prospective businesses against agreed criteria using public first-party sites or permitted directories, directly in COO's roster. Record business, public URL, location, fit reason, publicly evidenced software/workflow, source date and gaps. Do not scrape restricted data or infer a relationship. Support first approved attempts September 8–10. Cap the first batch's initial attempts at two working days, then support the next approved batch while follow-up continues; do not wait for every response or a complete model.
- Own the canonical funnel/cost register within the financial record; CMO supplies channel observations. Reconcile qualified targets, approved attempts, replies, conversations, demos, beta starts, activation, decisions and receipts. Link COO's value/renewal records rather than maintaining competing versions. Keep cohorts, periods and denominators consistent; expose duplicates and unknown attribution.
- Track direct channel spend and founder hours. Show cash acquisition cost and a separate time-adjusted cost using an explicit hourly-value assumption. With zero new paying customers, cost per acquired customer is not yet estimable; show spend, hours and intermediate outcomes instead. A tiny sample cannot establish durable CAC or lifetime value.
- Prepare small test-budget requests: hypothesis, target group, CMO-selected channel/message, sample limit, maximum cash at risk, founder hours, dates, success/stop conditions and evidence needed to decide. CEO approval comes before spend or contact. No account opening or paid subscription is implied.
- Prepare one partner business case when CMO identifies a relevant partner: reachable buyer overlap, net receipts, commission/refund exposure, onboarding/support effort, integration cost and downside. The CMO owns partner message and route; CEO authorizes outreach and terms.

**Deliverable:** validated roster fields, the canonical funnel/cost view and financial inputs to the existing approval packet for actual tests/partners. **Accepted when:** CMO can use the list, counts reconcile, and CEO sees exposure before approval. No separate weekly funnel sheet is required.

### CFO-08 — Measure full delivery cost and test the subscription economics

Prepare a cost map by September 14; first observed account-level comparison by September 21 where activity exists. Keep Copilot/Autopilot as the working subscription choices. Pricing remains a hypothesis to test through specific offers, owner value and cost evidence. Evaluate the optional prepayment proposal above; it changes no live billing until separately approved and implemented. Outcome-contingent billing remains deferred.

Measure model calls/fallbacks/retries, ingestion/posting, messages, shared infrastructure, payment fees, refunds, onboarding, ongoing support and founder delivery time. Distinguish cash cost from the imputed value of unpaid founder time. Record the hourly assumption and actual minutes; unpaid work is not economically free, but an imputed cost is not a bank outflow.

For the owner-operator v1 ([spec](../../2026-09-11-owner-operator/README.md)),
include the `owner_operator` planning calls within those model/tool costs,
and record founder minutes per delegated job as imputed labor. Report cost per correctly completed delegated job alongside
eligible/completed counts and account-level costs. Failed or abandoned
attempts and founder rescue stay in the numerator; zero completed jobs means
the ratio is unavailable, not zero cost. Separate setup from repeated
operation and make shared-cost allocation explicit. Use actual usage and
bounded scenarios, not unlimited chat inside the current subscription. No
billing change is implied.

When a real second-market decision exists, show the additional provider,
language-quality, onboarding and support costs using the same job definition.
The [market capability evidence](../../2026-08-26-verticals.md#market-capability-evidence)
defines operational readiness. Keep this analysis in the canonical CFO
register; the August vendor snapshot remains historical.

Separate account-level contribution, allocated shared service costs, acquisition costs and company overhead. Classify each item once in each reported view and provide a reconciliation: founder labor disclosed for unit economics must not then be charged again as extra cash; payroll already in the cash forecast is not added twice. Distinguish service-period revenue from cash collections, especially annual prepayments.

**Deliverable:** account/cohort cost fields and allocation notes in the canonical CFO register; add pricing comparisons only for an actual offer decision. **Accepted when:** activity links to costs, owner/founder effort are distinct, allocation is explained, and missing evidence produces “unknown.” Do not promise cents-per-location delivery or infer long-term retention from this beta.

### CFO-09 — Bring the cash decision forward, not past the deadline

### CFO-10 — Fund controlled repetition, October 8–November 6

This is an optional scenario, not a scheduled commitment. Use the master's gate and CEO decision before increasing spending or intake. Cleared cash must cover the authorized increment plus existing obligations through the next decision period; customer use/value and support capacity need evidence. ### CFO-11 — Allocate acceleration capital, November 7–December 6

Continued use, capacity and economics matter alongside customer count.

One hundred clients is an upside scenario, not the forecast or a committed target. Do not multiply a tiny pilot conversion rate into a promised acquisition curve. If the gate fails, focus on the smaller operation and the next credible cash decision.

**Deliverable:** staged allocation proposal and updated funding story. **Accepted when:** approved cash covers the exposure and the next increase depends on a stated, observable result.

## Weekly report and completion rule

Use exactly five lines. Each “done” item needs a dated artifact and evidence
the relevant founders can open; private records stay in the agreed private
location. No outside messages or automated reports are created by this brief.
Each task record uses the master's fields: ID, owner, due date, status, next
action, dependency, expected result, evidence link and decision required.
All statuses begin planned; select only the work needed for the current week.
The four founder roles are the workstreams. CFO task IDs are tasks within one
workstream, not additional workstreams or automatic separate debate events.

```text
Done: [Accepted task IDs and concrete results; use “none” when appropriate.]
Blocked: [Missing input/approval, owner, deadline and effect on the cash date.]
Next: [Up to three dated priorities within confirmed hours.]
Evidence: [Sanitized links to accepted outputs and their underlying authorized records.]
Risk: [Cash at risk, decision deadline, slipping milestone or untested assumption.]
```

At a workstream end, day-30/60/90 decision or material cash-strategy change,
provide the current plan and sanitized evidence for the coordinator's requested
two-round Claude challenge. Record objections, revisions and the CEO decision
before the next major commitment. Routine weekly reconciliation does not
imply that a workstream has ended or funding has succeeded.
