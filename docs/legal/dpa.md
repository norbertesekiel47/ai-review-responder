# Data Processing Addendum — counsel working draft

Version: 2026-09-16
Status: incomplete working draft; not executed or published as a customer agreement

This draft is for REPWORTH, INC., a Delaware corporation (RepWorth), and the business customer identified in an executed order (Customer). Complete the schedules, confirm operational capability, and obtain legal review before execution. There is intentionally no public `/legal/dpa` route. The public provider inventory is not a DPA. This document does not establish an international transfer mechanism or claim existing GDPR compliance.

## 1. Scope and roles

If executed, this addendum governs personal information RepWorth processes on Customer's behalf to provide the agreed Service. Customer is the controller or business and RepWorth is its processor or service provider for that information, as the applicable law defines those roles. Where Customer acts for another controller, Customer warrants authority to appoint RepWorth and pass on its instructions.

RepWorth's separate processing of its own account administration, billing, security, and legal-obligation records is described in the Privacy Policy. Roles must follow actual purposes and contractual arrangements; this clause does not reclassify processor data merely by calling it operational data.

## 2. Instructions and purpose limitation

RepWorth will process Customer Personal Data only on documented instructions in the executed agreement and the Customer's authorized feature settings, except as required by applicable law. Where legally permitted, RepWorth will inform Customer of a conflicting legal requirement or an instruction it considers unlawful. Customer is responsible for lawful collection, notices, channel permissions, and the instructions it gives.

Processing is limited to providing, maintaining, and securing the agreed Service. RepWorth will not sell Customer Personal Data, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or train general-purpose AI models on it. Where applicable service-provider law requires, RepWorth will not retain, use, or disclose it outside the specified business purposes or direct business relationship, or combine it with other sources except as legally permitted. RepWorth will notify Customer if it can no longer meet applicable contractual restrictions and cooperate with reasonable remediation steps.

## 3. Confidentiality and safeguards

Personnel authorized to process Customer Personal Data will be subject to confidentiality duties and appropriate access restrictions. RepWorth will maintain technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the processing risk, as documented in the completed security schedule. Provider certifications do not constitute a certification of RepWorth.

The schedule must identify implemented access controls, encrypted transport and verified at-rest protections, account/location isolation, approval controls, personnel access procedures, logging, incident response, backup/restore arrangements, deletion, and testing. The public Security page is a summary, not a substitute for the completed schedule.

## 4. Providers

Customer authorizes only the sub-processors identified in the completed schedule and used for the agreed features. RepWorth will impose appropriate written data-protection duties on those sub-processors and remain responsible as applicable law requires. Independent-controller providers and connected platforms must be identified separately.

Before a new or replacement sub-processor processes Customer Personal Data, RepWorth will give the advance notice agreed in the schedule and a meaningful opportunity to object on reasonable data-protection grounds. The parties will seek an alternative or mitigation. If no reasonable solution is available, Customer may terminate the affected processing as agreed in the completed commercial terms. The notice period, delivery method, and remedy remain open for negotiation; this draft does not establish a functioning notice-subscription service.

## 5. Assistance and incidents

Taking account of the nature of processing and information available, RepWorth will assist Customer with individual-rights requests, security obligations, impact assessments, and regulator consultations required by applicable law. RepWorth will promptly refer requests concerning Customer-controlled data to Customer unless otherwise legally required or instructed.

RepWorth will notify Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal-data breach affecting Customer Personal Data, provide available information about scope, likely consequences, and mitigation, and supplement it as investigation progresses. Notification is not an admission of liability. Customer remains responsible for its required notices to individuals and regulators; this does not excuse RepWorth's independent legal obligations. Confirm incident contacts and any negotiated time limit in the schedule.

## 6. Return and deletion

At Customer's choice at the end of processing, RepWorth will return or delete Customer Personal Data and delete copies, except where law requires retention. Agree the format, timing, and assistance for return before execution: the current product has no self-serve export. Retained data remains protected and limited to its lawful retention purpose.

The current account-deletion workflow stops processing and initiates provider teardown, with permanent purge scheduled after a 30-day waiting period. Provider failures can delay completion. The completed agreement must reconcile this workflow, backup lifetimes, suppression records, and independently retained billing records with applicable deletion duties. Standard subscription cancellation alone does not request deletion.

## 7. Assurance and audits

RepWorth will make available information reasonably needed to demonstrate compliance and allow and contribute to legally required audits by Customer or an appropriately bound independent auditor. Reasonable confidentiality, security, notice, and scheduling arrangements may protect other customers and systems, but must not defeat mandatory audit rights. Additional commercial assistance fees, if any, must not obstruct mandatory rights or breach-related cooperation.

## 8. International transfers

The parties must identify actual processing and remote-access countries, then establish any required lawful transfer mechanism before covered transfers occur. If EU Standard Contractual Clauses, a UK Addendum or IDTA, or another mechanism is needed, counsel must select and complete it with the appropriate parties, modules, annexes, authorities, and safeguards. No blank annex, link to a template, provider contract, or statement in this draft constitutes execution or coverage of the Customer-to-RepWorth transfer.

## 9. Priority and liability

Once executed, this addendum controls conflicting general terms concerning Customer Personal Data. Mandatory transfer clauses and applicable law prevail over conflicting provisions. Counsel must expressly reconcile the existing three-month fee liability cap with this addendum, breach exposure, mandatory liability, indemnities, and any negotiated cap; it is not silently expanded or validated by this draft.
