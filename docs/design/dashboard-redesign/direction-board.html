Console redesign — three directions

The same dashboard, the same modules, three candidate design systems. Structure follows the rules in dashboard-redesign.md: small type scale, strict grid, interpretation beside the data, one distinctive form (the Rep Pulse gauge), and a visible next action.

All numbers are sample data for comparison only. Hover the cards. Reload the page to replay the entry motion.

01Butter & Charcoal

Warm studio. Cream field, white cards, one charcoal panel that holds the day's action, butter accent. It keeps the brand's warmth and drops the serif quietness. Lowest risk, still a clear break from today.
