What owners ask first.

What can Autopilot post without asking me?+
Only replies to 4–5★ reviews that pass our safety checks and fit your Autopilot settings, and only after the veto window passes, the Judge is on duty, and your account isn’t paused. The Judge then reviews every one of them after it posts. Negative reviews are never auto-posted.
What happens if I don’t approve a draft?+
Nothing. Drafts wait in your dashboard. Email links expire after 48 hours, and if you go quiet for about three days with drafts waiting we send a reminder with a fresh link. If you never approve, we never post.
What about negative reviews?+
Guardian reads them first and grades how serious they are; the ones it decides need a human go straight to you. Drafts for 1–3★ reviews always wait for your approval. When Guardian rates an issue medium or high, the draft opens with an apology, avoids admitting fault, and invites the customer to continue offline.
How does it learn my voice?+
From day one, drafts follow your Brand Voice settings: tone, staff names, products, facts to mention or avoid. Once you have edited and posted five replies, RepWorth starts matching your most recent edits too: your tone, length, and phrasing.
What if the AI safety checks go down?+
Auto-posting stops. The Judge must be on duty for anything to post without your tap. If it can’t review, pending auto-approved replies fall back to your normal approval queue.
Which review platforms do you support?+
Today, Google only. That’s where most of the volume sits. Other review platforms are on the roadmap.
How do I connect my Google reviews?+
You connect your Google Business Profile with your Google login through our review partner. RepWorth never sees your Google password. Replies post as “Response from the owner”. One location is one Google Business Profile listing.
What about my existing reviews?+
When you connect, we offer to import your 100 most recent reviews from the past 12 months. You have 7 days to say yes. Imported reviews get no drafts unless you ask, and they are never auto-posted.
Where do approvals arrive?+
By email today. The link works for 48 hours, and your dashboard always works. Approve, edit, or regenerate (up to 3 times a day per review), with no login and no app. Reviews with a star rating but no text still get a reply.
Can I turn Autopilot off?+
Yes, any time in Settings, for your account or per location. If the Governor pauses it, you get an email and RepWorth support switches it back on after a review.
