# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: April 20, 2026**

Repworth ("we", "us", "our") operates the Repworth service at repworth.com. This policy describes what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

---

## 1. Data We Collect

- **Account data.** Your name, email address, and business name when you register.
- **Review data.** Google Business reviews associated with your business, fetched via SerpAPI on your behalf.
- **Response data.** AI-generated review response drafts and any edits you make before approving them.
- **Usage data.** Approval actions, response edits, and settings changes.
- **Payment data.** Handled entirely by Stripe. We store only your Stripe customer ID and subscription status — never raw card numbers.
- **SMS/mobile data.** If you opt in to SMS notifications during onboarding, we collect your mobile phone number solely to deliver review alerts and response approval notifications. This information is never shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Data

- To generate AI response drafts for your Google reviews.
- To send approval, notification, and weekly summary emails to your address.
- To send SMS notifications about new reviews and response drafts, if you have opted in.
- To process subscription billing via Stripe.
- To improve service reliability and detect misuse.

---

## 3. SMS Messaging Program

If you provide your mobile phone number and opt in during account registration, Repworth will send you SMS notifications including:

- Alerts when new Google reviews are received for your business.
- Notifications when AI-generated response drafts are ready for your approval.

**Consent:** Opt-in is obtained via an unchecked checkbox during account registration. By checking this box, you expressly consent to receive recurring automated text messages from Repworth. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

**Message frequency:** Message frequency varies based on your review volume, typically 1–10 messages per week.

**Rates:** Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier plan.

**Opt-out:** Reply **STOP** at any time to unsubscribe. You will receive a final confirmation message and no further SMS messages will be sent.

**Help:** Reply **HELP** for assistance or contact us at hello@repworth.com.

**Data sharing:** Mobile opt-in data and your mobile phone number will never be shared, sold, or rented to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

---

## 4. Third-Party Processors

| Processor            | Purpose                                                                   |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Google (via SerpAPI) | Review data retrieval                                                     |
| OpenAI / Anthropic   | AI response generation. Review text is sent to these APIs for processing. |
| Stripe               | Payment processing and subscription management                            |
| Resend               | Transactional email delivery                                              |
| Twilio               | SMS notification delivery                                                 |
| Supabase             | Database hosting. Data is stored in the United States.                    |

---

## 5. Data Retention

Account and review data is retained while your subscription is active. Upon cancellation or account deletion, personal data is removed within 30 days. Mobile phone numbers collected for SMS are removed within the same 30-day window. Anonymised aggregate statistics may be retained indefinitely to improve the service.

---

## 6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time — including your mobile number and SMS opt-in status. Email us at **hello@repworth.com** and we will respond within 30 days.

---

## 7. Security

All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest. Approval tokens are stored as one-way SHA-256 hashes and never in plaintext. Mobile phone numbers are stored in encrypted form and access is restricted to authorised systems only.

---

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We will notify registered users by email of material changes to this policy at least 14 days before they take effect.
