# Spec 17 — Legal & Compliance Docs

> **Goal:** ToS, Privacy Policy, AUP, DPA, Cookie Policy lawyered before charging first customer; AI-usage disclosure baked into product; GBP API ToS audit annually; insurance bound by first paid customer.

---

## 1. Why this matters

Legal exposure is asymmetric: drafts get you to MVP, but the first real dispute (chargeback, breach, regulatory inquiry) destroys hobbyist docs. Lawyering once costs $500–$1500; one bad inquiry costs $10k+. AI disclosure is a fast-moving regulatory landscape — California SB 942 and EU AI Act both bite in 2026.

## 2. Current state (RepWorth)

- `docs/Legal/repworth-privacy-policy.md` and `repworth-terms-of-service.md` exist as drafts.
- No AUP (Acceptable Use Policy).
- No DPA (Data Processing Addendum).
- No Cookie Policy.
- No `/legal/sub-processors` page.
- No AI usage disclosure visible in product.
- No insurance documented.

### Lawyer
- First-pass draft via **Termly** or **iubenda** (~$200/yr).
- Lawyered: $500–$1500 with SaaS-specialist (**Cooley GO templates**, **ClerkyHQ**, **Stripe Atlas legal**, or referred local).
- Re-review annually + after material business changes (new pricing model, new data class, new geography).

### Versioning
- Version-controlled in repo.
- Each version has `effective_date`.
- Material changes → prompt for re-accept in product (modal at login, log acceptance).
- Acceptance recorded in `legal_acceptances(user_id, doc_slug, version, accepted_at, ip, user_agent)`. Migration **052**.

### AI usage disclosure (regulatory triple-jurisdiction)
- **In ToS:** "Responses are AI-generated drafts requiring owner approval before publication. By using the Service, you understand and agree to this generation process."
- **In Privacy Policy:** disclosure that customer review data is sent to OpenAI / Anthropic for generation; data-handling terms.
- **In product:** at point-of-approval, banner reads "Draft generated by AI. Review carefully before approving."
- **Applicable laws (effective 2026):**
  - **EU AI Act** (Aug 2026): disclosure required for AI-generated content interacting with consumers.
  - **California SB 942** (Jan 2026): similar disclosure requirement.
- **Note**: the owner-approved-and-posted response is the *owner's speech* and need not be marked AI-generated to the reviewer. But the *generation process* must be disclosed to the customer (the SMB owner), which the above accomplishes.

### Google Business Profile API ToS
- Compliance audit annually. Critical clauses:
  - No auto-posting without owner consent ✅ (architecture invariant)
  - Rate limits respected
  - No caching beyond TTL
  - Attribution where required
  - No scraping (RepWorth uses GBP API + Zernio, not scraping)
- Documented in `docs/legal/gbp-compliance.md`.

### Insurance
- **By first paid customer**:
  - $1M E&O (Errors & Omissions / Professional Liability)
  - $1M Cyber liability
- Insurers (SaaS-friendly): **Vouch**, **Embroker**, **Coalition**.
- General liability follows when you have an office or employees.
- Required by most enterprise contracts.

### Refund & chargeback policy
- 30-day money-back guarantee on first paid month (already targeted in [Spec 02](./02-pricing-packaging.md)).
- Pro-rated refunds at founder discretion thereafter.
- Chargeback response template: `docs/runbooks/chargebacks.md`.

### Document retention
- 13-month minimum for audit logs ([Spec 04](./04-auth-identity.md)).
- 7-year for financial records (tax compliance).
- 90-day for LLM call logs (cost analysis + dispute).
- 7-day for raw request logs (then aggregate-only).

## 4. Implementation tasks

1. Pre-lawyer pass: use **Termly** or **iubenda** to draft AUP + DPA + Cookie Policy. Author ToS + PP refinements per latest business model.
2. Engage SaaS-specialist lawyer (Cooley GO or Stripe Atlas recommended). Brief: multi-location SMB SaaS, AI generation, Stripe billing, OpenAI/Anthropic sub-processors, US + EU/UK + AU + CA targets. Get markup back within 4 weeks.
3. Publish all docs at `/legal/*` routes. Render from `docs/Legal/*.md` via MDX.
4. Add legal-acceptance flow: migration 052 `legal_acceptances` table; modal at first-login-after-version-change.
5. Add `/legal/sub-processors` rendered table from `docs/Legal/sub-processors.md`. Setup mailing-list trigger for 30-day notification.
6. Add AI-disclosure banner at point-of-approval (`src/components/responses/ApprovalDrawer.tsx` or equivalent).
7. Author `docs/legal/gbp-compliance.md` and calendar annual review.
8. Bind insurance: $1M E&O + $1M Cyber via Vouch or Embroker. Record in `docs/legal/insurance.md`.
9. Add cookie-consent banner if EU traffic non-trivial. Use **Cookiebot** (low-cost) or hand-rolled.
10. Calendar entries: legal-review annually, GBP-ToS-review annually, insurance-renewal annually.

## 7. Risks & mitigations

- **Risk:** AI regulation changes between draft and ship. **Mitigation:** annual lawyer review; subscribe to EU AI Act + state AG newsletters; build the disclosure pattern into code so changes are copy edits.
- **Risk:** GBP ToS audit reveals non-compliance. **Mitigation:** architecture invariants already align; annual review catches drift.
- **Risk:** Insurance claim denied due to undisclosed risk class. **Mitigation:** complete underwriter questionnaire honestly; carry both E&O and Cyber.
