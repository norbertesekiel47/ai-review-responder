## 1. Why this matters

Legal exposure is asymmetric: drafts get you to MVP, but the first real dispute (chargeback, breach, regulatory inquiry) destroys hobbyist docs. Lawyering once costs $500–$1500; one bad inquiry costs $10k+. AI disclosure is a fast-moving regulatory landscape — California SB 942 and EU AI Act both bite in 2026.

### AI usage disclosure (regulatory triple-jurisdiction)
- **In ToS:** "Responses are AI-generated drafts requiring owner approval before publication. By using the Service, you understand and agree to this generation process."
- **In Privacy Policy:** disclosure that customer review data is sent to OpenAI / Anthropic for generation; data-handling terms.
- **In product:** at point-of-approval, banner reads "Draft generated by AI. Review carefully before approving."
- **Applicable laws (effective 2026):**
  - **EU AI Act** (Aug 2026): disclosure required for AI-generated content interacting with consumers.
  - **California SB 942** (Jan 2026): similar disclosure requirement.
- **Note**: the owner-approved-and-posted response is the *owner's speech* and need not be marked AI-generated to the reviewer. But the *generation process* must be disclosed to the customer (the SMB owner), which the above accomplishes.

### Google Business Profile API ToS

  - Rate limits respected

### Insurance
- **By first paid customer**:
  - $1M E&O (Errors & Omissions / Professional Liability)
  - $1M Cyber liability
- Insurers (SaaS-friendly): **Vouch**, **Embroker**, **Coalition**.
- General liability follows when you have an office or employees.
- Required by most enterprise contracts.

### Refund & chargeback policy
- 30-day money-back guarantee on first paid month (already targeted in [Spec 02](./02-pricing-packaging.md)).

### Document retention
- 13-month minimum for audit logs ([Spec 04](./04-auth-identity.md)).

## 7. Risks & mitigations

- **Risk:** AI regulation changes between draft and ship. **Mitigation:** annual lawyer review; subscribe to EU AI Act + state AG newsletters; build the disclosure pattern into code so changes are copy edits.
- **Risk:** GBP ToS audit reveals non-compliance. **Mitigation:** architecture invariants already align; annual review catches drift.
- **Risk:** Insurance claim denied due to undisclosed risk class. **Mitigation:** complete underwriter questionnaire honestly; carry both E&O and Cyber.
