# RepWorth Hero Video — Full Script + Storyboard (Blocks 1–13)

*Warm-tone pass. No em dashes. Narration validated against storyboard. Per-clause timing hints included for Blocks 4, 10, and 11.*

---

## Block 1 — Cold open (warm yellow)
- **Narration:** "Right now, somewhere, a customer is sharing their experience of your business, and the care you put into your reply is what the next customer feels before they ever walk in."
- **Storyboard:** A blank paper review card tilts in. An archival hand-with-pen cutout scribbles soft marker strokes on it. A gold paper star pops out and hangs glowing above. A second, dimmed card slides in from the right, gently waiting. Slow push-in on the star.

## Block 2 — The challenge, with empathy (off-white)
- **Narration:** "Keeping up with every review takes time most owners simply don't have, and each reply is a chance to show a customer they were truly heard."
- **Storyboard:** Review cards gather softly on a warm counter, gold stars still glowing. A friendly shopkeeper cutout reaches toward them with a pen, hands full happily serving a customer at the till. A small marker heart floats above the waiting cards, goodwill ready to be returned. Warm spotlight, no gloom, no pile.

## Block 3 — What RepWorth is (navy)
- **Narration:** "RepWorth isn't just another chatbot. It's a caring team of AI agents, each with one job, looking after your reputation around the clock."
- **Storyboard:** Gold star at center. Four paper badges snap into orbit: coral speech bubble (Reply), navy shield (Guardian), off-white bar chart (Insights), yellow megaphone (Solicitor). A white marker circle draws around the formation, and arrows connect each badge to the star.

## Block 4 — The four agents at work (warm yellow, four quadrants)
- **Narration:** "Reply crafts a warm answer for every review. Guardian flags the ones that deserve your personal touch. Insights writes your weekly digest. And Solicitor invites happy customers to share their story."
- **Storyboard:** Four torn-paper quadrants flip in, in the same order the narration names them. Speech-bubble badge stamps a finished reply card. Shield badge gently catches a tricky card and raises a small flag for you. Chart badge sits beside a line drawing upward. Megaphone badge sends envelopes out while tiny gold stars float back. The gold star rests at the center crossing point.

**Per-clause timing (total ≈ 13.5s, timecodes relative to block start):**

| Time | Clause (subtitle) | Sub-shot enters |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:03.5 | "Reply crafts a warm answer for every review." | Speech-bubble badge stamps the finished reply card |
| 0:03.5–0:07.0 | "Guardian flags the ones that deserve your personal touch." | Shield badge catches the tricky card, raises the small flag |
| 0:07.0–0:09.5 | "Insights writes your weekly digest." | Chart badge, line drawing upward |
| 0:09.5–0:13.5 | "And Solicitor invites happy customers to share their story." | Megaphone badge fires envelopes out, gold stars float back |

*Quadrants 1 and 2 need the most time (longest clauses); keep the "Insights" quadrant animation snappy so it doesn't sit idle.*

## Block 5 — The watchdogs (navy)
- **Narration:** "And watching over it all are two guardians. A Judge that reviews one hundred percent of automatic posts, and a Governor that can pause the system the instant you want."
- **Storyboard:** The star and badges formation sits below, warmly lit. Two larger cutouts descend on tape strips: first a gavel (Judge), then a vintage toggle switch (Governor). The gavel taps once and a soft golden ring ripples outward. The toggle hovers, lever poised, a friendly marker arrow pointing to it.

## Block 6 — Copilot vs Autopilot (coral)
- **Narration:** "You're always in control. Copilot lets you approve every reply yourself, and Autopilot sends the safe, friendly ones for you, inside limits you set."
- **Storyboard:** A big hand-drawn dial arcs across frame, gold star at its pivot. On the left, an archival hand presses an approval stamp onto a card (Copilot). On the right, reply cards fly out on their own but stay inside a hand-drawn dashed boundary (Autopilot guardrails). The needle sweeps left, then right, then settles in the middle.

## Block 7 — Safety & audit trail (off-white)
- **Narration:** "Nothing goes public without your say-so, anything sensitive is set gently aside for you, and every step is recorded, so you can always see what happened, and why."
- **Storyboard:** The gold star sits inside a torn-paper vault. First, a tricky card approaches and a soft navy bar slides over it, and it is set behind a holding line with tape, held safely and never sent (this matches "anything sensitive is set gently aside"). Then paper strips file one by one into a tidy ledger, the run spine, each landing with a marker tick (this matches "every step is recorded").

## Block 8 — The turn (warm yellow)
- **Narration:** "Today, RepWorth already runs the whole loop for you, from inviting brand-new reviews to publishing warm replies. And this is only the beginning."
- **Storyboard:** The star and its four badges sit in a tight inner orbit. Concentric dashed rings draw outward past the frame, dotted with open slots waiting to be filled. A steady pull-back reveals the room to grow around the small existing fleet.

## Block 9 — The fleet grows (navy)
- **Narration:** "Soon the team grows, with agents to nurture your customer relationships, care for your social media, and stand guard against fake reviews across every platform."
- **Storyboard:** Three new badges snap into the outer ring, in narration order: coral rolodex/contact card (CRM), heart and comment bubbles (Social), magnifying glass over a fake-looking card that tears in half (Fake-review dispute). Then, along the bottom, platform tiles deal out like cards, each linked to the star by a thin line (across every platform).

## Block 10 — New intelligence (coral)
- **Narration:** "New insight arrives too, showing how you shine in your neighborhood, turning reviews into simple improvements, and protecting you the moment an unfair wave of reviews appears."
- **Storyboard:** Three beats matching the narration's three ideas. Left, a flat street map with gentle pulsing dots, your bright gold star among a few neutral gray stars, a friendly marker circle around yours ("how you shine in your neighborhood"). Center, a review card transforms into a small checklist as marker ticks stamp down its lines ("turning reviews into simple improvements"). Right, a line chart dips as a few dark cards drift in, the navy shield settles calmly over it, and the line eases back to steady ("protecting you the moment an unfair wave of reviews appears").

**Per-clause timing (total ≈ 11.5s, timecodes relative to block start):**

| Time | Clause (subtitle) | Sub-shot enters |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:01.5 | "New insight arrives too," | Frame settles, gold star centered (lead-in, no new element yet) |
| 0:01.5–0:04.5 | "showing how you shine in your neighborhood," | Left: street map, your bright star among gray stars, marker circle |
| 0:04.5–0:07.0 | "turning reviews into simple improvements," | Center: review card transforms into a checklist, marker ticks stamp down |
| 0:07.0–0:11.5 | "and protecting you the moment an unfair wave of reviews appears." | Right: line dips, dark cards drift in, navy shield settles, line eases back to steady |

*The protection beat is the longest clause and the most motion, so it gets the widest window. Do not start the shield until "protecting."*

## Block 11 — Growth levers (off-white)
- **Narration:** "Others keep your profile fresh and easy to find, ask for reviews at just the right moment, celebrate the staff your customers love, and build a reputation score you'll be proud to share."
- **Storyboard:** Four small vignettes around the star, in narration order. A storefront card gets a shine wipe and a fresh photo taped on (keep your profile fresh). A paper clock ticks to a marked moment and launches one envelope (ask at the right moment). Worker cutouts in aprons each receive a tiny gold star with a warm praise circle (celebrate the staff). Everything converges into one large gold star that stamps down like a wax seal (reputation score).

**Per-clause timing (total ≈ 12.5s, timecodes relative to block start):**

| Time | Clause (subtitle) | Sub-shot enters |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:03.3 | "Others keep your profile fresh and easy to find," | Storefront card gets the shine wipe and fresh photo taped on |
| 0:03.3–0:06.3 | "ask for reviews at just the right moment," | Paper clock ticks to the marked moment, launches one envelope |
| 0:06.3–0:09.0 | "celebrate the staff your customers love," | Worker cutouts each receive a tiny gold star with a praise circle |
| 0:09.0–0:12.5 | "and build a reputation score you'll be proud to share." | All four vignettes converge into the large gold star wax seal |

*The wax-seal convergence in the final clause should gather the previous three vignettes, so hold them faintly on screen until the seal stamps, then let them collapse inward.*

## Block 12 — Finale / kicker (navy, warming to gold)
- **Narration:** "One day, agencies will care for entire rosters this way, because RepWorth is the always-supervised reputation team every small business deserves."
- **Storyboard:** The camera cranes up from a tight star close-up to the full constellation: gold star at center, the badge fleet orbiting in two rings, gavel and toggle stationed above, and storefronts along the bottom, each with its own tiny star (the agency roster). One continuous marker stroke draws a protective circle around everything, and the star pulses once, brightly and warmly.

## Block 13 — Logo sign-off (navy warming to gold, then clean brand frame)
- **Narration:** *(silent hold, optional single line below)*
- **On-screen tagline (optional):** "RepWorth. The reputation team every business deserves."
- **Storyboard:** From Block 12's constellation, all orbiting badges draw inward along their marker lines and collapse into the central gold star with one warm pulse. The star's light blooms and resolves into the RepWorth logo, the paper-collage texture smoothing into the clean brand mark. A final marker underline sweeps beneath it, and the tagline fades in below. Hold, one last soft golden shimmer, then settle. Clean brand-color background.
